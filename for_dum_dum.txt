Bayesian Poisson is the half of your model that thinks in goals rather than win/draw/loss. It's where all those λ (lambda) values come from.

Start with Poisson. The Poisson distribution models "how many of a rare-ish event happen in a fixed window" — phone calls per hour, typos per page, and famously goals per match. It takes one input, λ (the expected/average count), and gives you the probability of 0 goals, 1 goal, 2 goals, etc. λ=1.8 means "on average 1.8 goals, most likely 1 or 2." This is why your scorelines cluster low — that's just what Poisson with λ≈1–2 looks like.
